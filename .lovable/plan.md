# Bright, modern visual refresh

## What will change
- Replace the black, dark navy background with a clean white and cool-gray foundation.
- Keep EA Properties Solution and RealEstateJagaban branding prominent with deep navy text and crisp logo framing.
- Restyle navigation, page surfaces, property cards, forms, footer, and floating contact controls for the light theme.
- Soften the background effects into subtle architectural lines and restrained blue highlights rather than dark gradients.
- Preserve the existing pages, content, animations, booking flow, WhatsApp contact, and admin functionality.

## Technical details
- Update the shared semantic color, border, shadow, and background tokens so all screens inherit the brighter design consistently.
- Remove component-level dark-only colors where they reduce readability in the new theme.
- Keep contrast accessible and verify the homepage on the current mobile view and desktop.
